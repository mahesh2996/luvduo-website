FAQ

Good questions

Do we both need the app?

Yes — BaeNest is made for exactly two. Each of you installs it on your own phone, then you pair once.

We’re long-distance. Does it still work?

Yes. Send your invite on WhatsApp to pair, and heartbeats and moments reach your partner wherever they are.

Who can see what I share?

What you share in your nest is for your paired partner. See our privacy policy for the details.

How does the nest grow?

Each day you both finish your heartbeat, questions and oath, your nest gets a new twig.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android comes first. iPhone is on the way.
